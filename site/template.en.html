Roadmap

  1. Permissionless ratingScore from public data and publish. Not taking part becomes information in itself.
  2. Sources and correctionsEvery score links its sources. Anyone can correct it with a source and 2 approvals. We are here now.
  3. Registered promisesProjects register their return, selling and unlock commitments in a fixed format.
  4. Collateralized promisesProjects post collateral that goes to holders, by on-chain rules, if a promise is broken.

Out of scope: hack prevention, market moves, and whether there is demand. Token Charter rates the reliability of promises only.

Scores are based on public information and change only through sourced proposals with 2 approvals. Not investment advice.
